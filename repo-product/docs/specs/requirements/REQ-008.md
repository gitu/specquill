# Conflict-checked merges to the default branch

A workspace branch reaches the protected default branch through a
conflict-checked merge. Review, when a team wants it, happens on the forge —
see [merging.md](../specs/merging.md).

> **REQ-008.1 · MUST** — A merge into a protected branch SHALL be the only
> way content reaches it; direct writes SHALL remain refused
> ([REQ-001](REQ-001.md)).

> **REQ-008.2 · MUST** — Before merging, the app SHALL report what would
> land: the diff, whether it conflicts, and any uncommitted work on the
> source branch.

> **REQ-008.3 · MUST** — A merge SHALL be refused while the source worktree
> has uncommitted changes, so a merge never lands less than was previewed.

> **REQ-008.4 · MUST** — Merges SHALL detect conflicts and refuse rather than
> produce a conflicted tree; the reference SHALL advance atomically so
> concurrent merges cannot clobber each other.

> **REQ-008.5 · MUST** — Merging SHALL require the `member` role; a `viewer`
> MAY preview a merge but SHALL NOT perform one.

> **REQ-008.6 · SHOULD** — The commit SHALL record the merging user as author
> and committer, with the service identity and any collaborators as
> `Co-authored-by:` trailers.
