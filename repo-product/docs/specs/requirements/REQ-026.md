# Timed dependencies from document validity windows

A document whose frontmatter carries a **validity window** — a start date, an
end date, or both — becomes a **timed dependency**: something the workspace
owes the world by a date. The workspace derives, per window, whether it has
opened yet, whether it is closing, and whether everything that depends on it
is finished in time. This replaces the change-record inbox, which asked
authors to write documents *about* change instead of letting the documents
carry their own dates.

> **REQ-026.1 · MUST** — Which frontmatter keys carry a window SHALL be
> workspace configuration (`timed:` in `.specquill/config.yml`), not a fixed
> vocabulary: an ordered list of start keys and of end keys, of which the
> FIRST present on a document wins. The built-in defaults SHALL accept both
> plain (`starts`, `ends`) and regulatory (`effective_from`,
> `effective_until`, `valid_from`, `valid_until`, `due`) wording, so an
> existing workspace joins the timeline without editing its documents.

> **REQ-026.2 · MUST** — Any document of any family MAY be a timed
> dependency; membership is decided by carrying a configured key, never by
> living in a designated folder. A workspace MAY narrow the timeline to named
> families (`kinds:`), and a document carrying no configured key SHALL NOT
> appear on it.

> **REQ-026.3 · MUST** — Each timed document SHALL be classified against the
> current date into exactly one state: **pending** (its start is in the
> future), **active** (started, end far enough away), **expiring** (its end
> falls inside the configured horizon) or **expired** (its end has passed).
> The horizon SHALL be configurable (`horizon_days`, default 90).

> **REQ-026.4 · MUST** — For every timed document the workspace SHALL roll up
> the documents that depend on it — the inbound typed links of
> [REQ-012](REQ-012.md)'s model — and count how many of them have reached a
> configured ready status (`ready_statuses`, default `approved`/`done`).

> **REQ-026.5 · MUST** — A timed document SHALL be flagged **at risk** when
> its window opens (or closes) inside the horizon while it or any document
> depending on it is not ready. At-risk items SHALL be surfaced without the
> user opening the timeline: on the Overview's review list and as a count on
> the navigation rail.

> **REQ-026.6 · SHOULD** — The timeline SHOULD be reachable as its own view
> with the four states as filters and a shareable per-document URL, and each
> item SHOULD show its window, its own status, and the readiness of every
> dependent document.

> **REQ-026.7 · MUST** — A workspace where no document carries a window SHALL
> degrade to nothing: no tiles, no cards, no empty counters — only an
> explanation of which keys would light the view up.
